# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-04)

The first release.

- **Polling rate tester:** current, average and peak report rate, and the closest standard rate.
  Every report is timed by the kernel, so readings aren't capped at the screen's refresh rate.
- **Mouse rotation:** swipe side to side and get the sensor rotation that levels your swipes, with
  a margin of error and a trail of your swipes.
- Click to capture the mouse; **Escape** or **right-click** stops.
- No permissions at all.
